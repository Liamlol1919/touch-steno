# Consolidated kill register

**Project status:** 22 candidates were generated in the design sprint, 0 were validated, and **0 signals survive** because the last positive result was withdrawn by a permutation-null test. Here, `n=22` means candidate records, human `n=0` operators and `0` sessions. **A score is not a viability result.** A score orders or criticises a proposal; it cannot compensate for an observability veto, a failed safety gate, or missing human evidence.

This register is a rename barrier as well as a history. A branch killed here remains killed under a new name unless a record explicitly changes its primitive, removes the recorded dependency, and passes the stated falsifier. “Suspended” and “hold” are not soft approvals: no implementation, promotion, or evidence claim follows from them.

## Evidence conventions

- A `/50` or `/100` review score below is, unless stated otherwise, a score for `n=1` named candidate, with human `n=0` operators and `0` sessions; operator and session are therefore not applicable. It is not a human measurement.
- A measured human result states its trial/frame/stroke `n`, operator count, session count and capture caveat in the same table cell.
- A synthetic result states event and session `n`, with `0` human operators. Synthetic outputs are generated-data facts, not device or human results.
- A corpus result states token `n`, with `0` operators and `0` sessions. Corpus arithmetic is method- and corpus-dependent.
- Where sources disagree, both values and both provenance are retained. No score is silently harmonised.

## Retractions in force

The following numbers or premises are withdrawn. A later corrected number does not rehabilitate the original wording.

1. **The 88% language-recovery claim.** The “6.8% to 88.8%” result came from `n=1,200` generated words per radius and a `n=20,000`-word list, with `0` human operators and `0` hardware sessions. Candidate generation was conditioned on the intended sector, which leaked the answer. The correctly conditioned observed-sector experiment is the record to use; the retracted table, correction load and derived typing-rate arithmetic must not be quoted. See `LM_RECOVERY.md` retraction and `FIVE_HOURS_REPORT.md` §3.
2. **The Fitts “impossible” overreach.** The withdrawn claim used provisional coefficients with `n=0` fitted hand trials, `0` operators and `0` sessions. It bounds target-to-target aiming, not every drawn-path primitive. Do not generalise it into a device or human-performance impossibility claim. See `BINDING_CONSTRAINT.md` §1.
3. **The 22.7% bimanual multiplication claim.** This withdrawn arithmetic has no recoverable sample size, operator or session provenance in the current root records; those missing fields are themselves the reason it cannot be cited. The defensible bimanual record says the relevant controlled evidence is missing or marginal. See `W6_THUMB_BIMANUAL_VIABILITY.md` §3 and `CUED_SESSION_PROTOCOLS.md`.
4. **The correction-cost premise.** “Lower accuracy plus cheap repair beats higher accuracy plus expensive repair” does not hold when repair consumes another event. This was design arithmetic with human `n=0`, `0` operators and `0` sessions; the repair-time inputs were assumptions, not measurements. The advantage exists only for repair inside the same event. See `BINDING_CONSTRAINT.md` §2.8.
5. **The throughput formula.** The naive `(1−p)/A` accepted-output formula omits failed-candidate occupancy. Its replacement is geometric-attempt accounting, but the example inputs have human `n=0`, `0` operators and `0` sessions and are not a user result. See `BINDING_CONSTRAINT.md` §§3.3 and 3.6.
6. **The incorrect `2^9.476` result and its companion exponentials.** The withdrawn values `716.8` for English and `1071.6` for German, plus the later inconsistent German approximation `1024.9`, must not be quoted. The corpus arithmetic used `n=725,119,374` English tokens and `n=151,705,378` German tokens, `0` operators and `0` sessions; its retained corrections are `2^9.476 = 712.30` and `2^10.024 ≈ 1041.42`. These are corpus/codebook calculations, not input or human results. See `BINDING_CONSTRAINT.md` §§3.1, 3.3 and 10.1.
7. **The syllable typing-rate arithmetic.** The first shape-pair estimate and the later complete ladder are withdrawn as user-facing arithmetic because event-rate ceilings, segmentation assumptions and syllable inventories were combined without human validation. Human `n=0`, `0` operators and `0` sessions. Only the corpus-derived structural event-count finding may be cited with its corpus and weighting caveats; no user-facing typing-rate result exists. See `BINDING_CONSTRAINT.md` §§3.9–3.12 and 10.
8. **The German/English weighting mismatch.** The “English is materially easier” comparison is provisional: the English figures are token-weighted, while the German figure may be unweighted. The underlying corpora contain `n=725,119,374` English tokens and `n=151,705,378` German tokens, with `0` operators and `0` sessions. Until matched recomputation exists, do not quote the size of the cross-language gap. See `BINDING_CONSTRAINT.md` §10.2.
9. **The permutation-invariant field descriptor's `0.4583` result — the ninth and final-positive retraction.** The number existed as an observed class-mean top-1, but it is withdrawn, not replaced. Across 1,000 shuffled-label permutations (seed 20260925, descriptor dimension 57), the observed **0.4583** was below the null mean **0.5685**; the null median was **0.5833**, its 95th percentile **0.7083**, its 99th percentile **0.7500**, and the one-sided `p=0.9460`. The prespecified withdrawal rule was `p >= 0.05`, so this result is killed. The descriptor was fitted at dimension 57 to only `n=24` measured cued trials, 8 compass classes, 3 per class, from one operator in one session; the wider capture had `n=63` strokes, thumb-compass labels and no error bars. Because 94.6% of permutations scored at or above the real result, it provides no evidence of class information and may not be revived under a new descriptor name.
