retain either side of the former merge conflict.

Read these documents for the current decision and its constraints:

1. [`DESIGN_DECISION_FINAL.md`](DESIGN_DECISION_FINAL.md) — the final design comparison and

These are the authoritative current records. If this index or an older document conflicts
with either record, the records above control.

## Superseded direction

The earlier **direction-quantisation frame**—classifying thumb movement into compass
sectors and tuning its radius, sector count, or antipodal fold—is **superseded and
closed**. It is not the current design direction. The surviving design work and decisions
are recorded in `DESIGN_DECISION_FINAL.md` and `BINDING_CONSTRAINT.md`; no candidate from
the old frame should be revived by citing this compatibility file.

## History and archival record

[`ARCHIVE.md`](ARCHIVE.md) is the document-set index and audit trail. It records which
materials remain load-bearing, which are current, and which are retracted or superseded.
The old documents and competing formulations remain preserved there; nothing in this index
deletes that history. Consult the archive when tracing a superseded claim, correction, or
design iteration.
