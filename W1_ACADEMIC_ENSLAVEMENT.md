## 10 most useful papers for building a velocity/coupling-based intent filter

1. Zatsiorsky, Li, Latash 2000, Exp Brain Res 131:187–195. “Enslaving effects in multi-finger force production.” DOI:10.1007/s002219900261. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10766271/ — Foundational enslaving matrix + 67.5% bound.
2. Martin, Latash, Zatsiorsky 2009, Exp Brain Res 192:293–298. “Interaction of finger enslaving and error compensation…” PMCID:PMC2648126. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2648126/ — Full r and slope matrices vs initial force / delta; the prior for expected slave velocity given master velocity at rest vs preload.
3. Hager-Ross & Schieber 2000, J Neurosci 20(22):8542–8550. “Quantifying the independence of human finger movements.” DOI:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.20-22-08542.2000. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6773164/ — II/SI tables, finger ranking, 2 Hz vs 3 Hz effect.
4. Danion, Latash, Li, Zatsiorsky 2000, J Physiol 523:523–532. “The effect of fatigue on multifinger co-ordination…” PMCID:PMC2269799. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2269799/ — Force deficit 12.7%→25.4%, enslaving N and %, sharing stability; session-adaptation numbers.
5. Park, Xu et al. 2017, Front Hum Neurosci 11:318. “Multi-finger interaction and synergies in flexion and extension.” DOI:10.3389/fnhum.2017.00318. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2017.00318/full — Flexion 102 N vs extension 56 N MVCs, per-finger MVCs, EN ordering, ASA −200–300 ms review.
6. Bi, Li, Zhai 2013, CHI. “FFitts law: modeling finger touch with Fitts’ law.” https://research.google/pubs/ffitts-law-modeling-finger-touch-with-fitts-law/ (+ σ_a=0.94 mm 1-D value via https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10337563) — Spatial noise floor σ_a and dual-Gaussian endpoint model; R²≥0.91.
7. Holz & Baudisch 2010, CHI. “The Generalized Perceived Input Point Model and how to double touch accuracy by extracting fingerprints.” https://www.christianholz.net/2010-chi10-holz-baudisch-the_generalized_perceived_input_point_model_and_how_to_double_touch_accuracy_by_extracting_fingerprints.pdf and https://siplab.org/projects/Generalized_Perceived_Input_Point_Model — Systematic per-posture offset (few mm), 67% error explained, 2–3.3× gain; justifies per-finger calibration.
8. Schwarz, Xiao, Mankoff, Hudson, Harrison 2014, CHI. “Probabilistic palm rejection using spatiotemporal touch features and iterative classification.” DOI:10.1145/2556288.2557056. https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2556288.2557056 (PDF https://robertxiao.ca/pubs/2014_CHI_Palm-Rejection.pdf; page https://www.chrisharrison.net/index.php/Research/PalmRejection) — 0.016/stroke, 98% pass; directly reusable temporal-evolution classifier design.
9. Valero-Cuevas 1998, J Biomech 31:693–703. “Large index-fingertip forces are produced by subject-independent patterns of muscle excitation.” https://valerolab.org/Papers/Valero-CuevasJOB1998.pdf (also https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9796669/) — Directional maxima (palmar 27.9 N … dorsal 7.5 N); scales micro-shear intent vs max.
10. Umezu et al., touchscreen fingertip forces & completion time (tap 0.50±0.09 N; slide/pinch/stretch ladder; tap 133±83 ms). Abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28314216/ and record https://www.researchgate.net/publication/314021838_Fingertip_forces_and_completion_time_for_index_finger_and_thumb_touchscreen_gestures — Lightest deliberate touch ≈0.5 N and fastest tap ≈130 ms; empirical floor for “deliberate” on capacitive surfaces.

## Deliberate NOT-FOUND list (do not use as design constants)

- Optimal finger count for chorded keying; Kapur MVP pressure value; single “independence %”; healthy resting-on-surface jitter in mm or mm/s; passive drift on touch surfaces; minimum detectable displacement/velocity on touchscreens (mm, mm/s); Nenya/ring deg/mm thresholds; DragSense/PinchZoom/Velocity-Touch mm/s cutoffs; validated 0-force pressure/area/hover/dwell intent cutoff for text entry; academic capacitive finger-hover height.
